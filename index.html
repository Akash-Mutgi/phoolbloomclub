Phool Bloom Club was born from a simple want: to send something real. Something you hold in your hands, made slowly and with intention — original art, a handwritten letter, and a genuine thread of connection between the person who made it and the person who opens it.

Phool means flower in Hindi. Like a flower, this club is rooted in something slow, patient, and alive.

Questions

Everything you're
wondering about.

What is the Phool Bloom Club?+
Phool Bloom Club is a monthly snail mail subscription by an illustrator. Each month, members receive a handcrafted envelope filled with original art prints, stickers, a postcard, a personal letter, and a surprise creative activity — all centred around a flower of the month. Phool means flower in Hindi.
When do subscriptions open?+
Subscriptions are open now — choose your plan and subscribe whenever you like. Envelopes ship on the 1st of every month, so if you join mid-month your first envelope goes out on the next 1st.
How much does a subscription cost?+
₹555/month for India subscribers and ₹1100/month for international subscribers. Both tiers include the same curated envelope contents. Shipping is included in the price.
What's included in every envelope?+
Every Phool envelope includes: an A6 original art print, an A7 mini art print, a sticker sheet, a postcard, a personal handwritten letter, and one surprise fun activity (such as a colouring page or DIY craft).
When does the envelope ship?+
Envelopes ship on the 1st of each month. Indian subscribers receive theirs in 3–5 days; international members in 10–20 days.
Can I cancel my subscription?+
Yes, cancel anytime. No lock-in periods. Cancellations before the 25th of the month won't be charged or shipped for the following month.
Is this available as a gift?+
Absolutely. A Phool Bloom Club subscription is a beautiful gift for artists, stationery lovers, and anyone who appreciates slow, thoughtful things. Gift subscriptions are coming soon.
