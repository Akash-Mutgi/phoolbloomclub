Your first envelope

Envelopes go out on the 1st of every month. If you joined mid-month, yours is posted on the next 1st — not immediately, because each one is packed by hand in a single batch.

India: 3–5 days once posted. International: 10–20 days.

Where we'll post it

We'll send it to the delivery address you entered at checkout — that exact address, including the postcode and phone number, is what goes on the label and what the courier uses to reach you.

Spotted a mistake, or moved? Write to hello@phoolbloomclub.com before the 25th and we'll update it for the next envelope.

What to expect each month

  • One flower of the month, running through every piece inside
  • An A6 art print, an A7 mini print, a sticker sheet and a postcard
  • A handwritten letter from Avnie, and one surprise activity
  • Cancel anytime — no lock-in. Cancel before the 25th and the following month isn't charged or shipped.
